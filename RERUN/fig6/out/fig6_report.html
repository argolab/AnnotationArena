Domain 3 · corrected replication

Figure 6, rebuilt on the test corner

The published panels score Marformer on every missing cell in the graph while Stan is scored on the 414-cell corner. These are rebuilt on the corner for both arms, with the non-transductive curve extended to the full item sweep.

What changed

Two corrections · one extension

1. The evaluation set (D22/D23). test.py builds train_obs + test_obs + test_miss + train_miss and averages every missing token, while Stan is scored on missing_ratings_indexes_in_test_instance alone. The paper says the corner is the target: “All reported log-losses are evaluated on missing entries within this corner.” At K=4000 only 2.9% of the published number is corner.

2. Checkpoint selection (D14). Published points are an argmin over ~18 checkpoints scored on test. These use block-validation: carve, select the stopping epoch on the carve, refit on the whole training partition. Test is never consulted and the refit’s data budget equals Stan’s.

3. The NT curve now spans the full sweep. It stopped at K=900 (the plot script capped it at 500); the transductive arm ran to 4000.

Stan is absent. No Domain 3 sigmoid Stan results survived the migration to skipjack — the only Stan outputs present are for other domains. The series is omitted rather than approximated.

The panels

Corner metric · 3 seeds · solid = block-val + refit, dotted = last checkpoint

Shaded bands are the standard deviation across 3 seeds. They understate the true uncertainty: the corner is a fixed 414 cells, so any measurement carries about ±0.06 of cell-sampling noise at 95%, and more seeds cannot reduce it because every seed scores the same cells (D27). Point-to-point moves under 0.06 are not signal.

The annotator NT curve cannot be extended past 40: domain3subsplit.py enforces train_size ≤ total_annots − test_annotators, and the base has J=50 with 10 reserved for the corner. Annot_T_50 and Annot_NT_40 both train on 40 annotators, so the published curve ending at 40 is a property of the data, not an omission.

How to read it

Three things the corrected panels show that the published ones do not

  • Marformer is 0.05–0.19 nats worse than published, at every size. Two causes stack: the metric was mostly training cells, and the checkpoint was chosen on test. Since Stan was always scored on the corner with no selection, the gap to Stan closes by that amount.
  • The spike at K=600 is a chunking artifact (D24), not the model. It lands exactly where the graph first needs a second chunk at max_item=300. Raising the chunk size to 500 moves it to K=900 rather than removing it — the fix is to keep the corner whole and in a chunk with training context.
  • The NT arm diverges past K=900 rather than converging on T (D25). It fits training cells identically to the transductive arm (0.7659 vs 0.7664 at K=4000) but cannot transfer, and its corner loss bottoms near epoch 100 then climbs ~0.13. Even early-stopped at its own optimum it stays ~0.12 above T and is not converging, so the paper’s claim that NT is “comparable at large training sizes” — never tested past K=500 — does not hold. Halving or quartering the learning rate does not change it.

The NT points at K ≥ 1000 are protocol-limited rather than model-limited: block-validation stops at ~epoch 313 while the corner optimum is ~124, because a validation set carved from the training partition cannot see a transfer failure to the corner (D26).
